## What the site is
iStockVisual is a stock-visual library plus in-browser studio tools.
Library: photos, illustrations, vectors, and 3D stills. Search, filter, download.
Most library images are generated or prepared for this site and released under CC0.
Hosting: Cloudflare. Accounts in D1. Files and short-lived visual-search uploads in R2.

## Downloads and sizes
Free: 500 and 1K previews. No paid plan required.
Pro / pass: 2K, 4K, and 8K while the plan is active.
Formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP, SVG (depends on the asset). Same license on every size.
When a term plan expires: keep files already downloaded; new 2K/4K/8K lock until renew or Lifetime. 500 and 1K stay free.

## Fair use
First 100 HD (2K/4K/8K) downloads each day: unrestricted.
After that: 10 HD downloads per hour, to keep speed up and block scraping.
We may tighten/pause/restore limits if we see abuse or outages.

## Refunds
Refund rules depend on the plan.
6-Month Pro, 12-Month Pro, and Lifetime Access: 7-day free trial from purchase. No-questions-asked refund within 7 days, even if you downloaded images. After 7 days: no refund except where law requires it.
1-Month Pro: 3-day trial. Refund within those 3 days only if unused (no library image downloads after purchase). After 3 days, or after any download, no refund.
1-Day Pass and 7-Day Pass: no calendar money-back window. Unused only (no downloads after purchase). A single download voids the refund. Free-member downloads from before that paid purchase do not count.
How: email hello@istockvisual.com from the account address. Include account email, plan name, approximate purchase date, optional note.
After refund: that Pro/pass access ends; free 500/1K remains; files already downloaded stay usable under the license (attribution still required).
Chargebacks: contact us first. Repeated refund abuse or fraud can lead to refused goodwill refunds or account suspension.
Page: /info/refunds
